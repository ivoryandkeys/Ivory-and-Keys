THE ART OF MUSIC

Welcome to Ivory & Keys. A musical journey built around emotion, creativity and the beauty of sound.

CP Botha - " Born and raised in a small town in the Free State, South Africa, my journey with music began at a young age. What started as a simple passion gradually grew into something far greater – a deep love for the art of creating, expressing and connecting through music. To me, music has always been more than just notes and melodies; it is a language of its own, capable of expressing emotions that words sometimes cannot. Over the years, the piano has become my greatest creative outlet, allowing me to explore different sounds, develop my own style and bring musical ideas to life. I find inspiration in the beauty of melodies, the emotion behind a composition and the endless possibilities that come with creating something entirely my own. Whether through original compositions or my own interpretations of familiar music, my goal is always to create something meaningful – something that makes people pause, feel and connect. Ivory & Keys is a reflection of that passion. It represents my journey as a musician, my love for the piano and my desire to share the music I create with the world. Every composition tells a story, and every performance is an opportunity to share a little piece of that story with someone else. This is only the beginning, and I look forward to seeing where the music takes me."

From cinematic piano compositions to expressive live performances, every note tells a story.
